How to get started

1

Download for your platform above

Windows: unzip and run BestBrand.exe · Mac: open DMG and drag to Applications · Linux: extract and run ./BestBrand

2

Complete the one-time setup

On first launch you'll verify your age (18+) and agree to the Terms of Use. This is recorded locally on your device.

3

Fill in your API keys

Go to the Settings tab and enter your Hyperliquid wallet key, Telegram bot token, and any prediction market API keys.

4

Start the webhook server

Click ▶ Start Server in the sidebar. Point your TradingView alerts at your server URL.

5

Approve trades from Telegram

Every signal sends you a Telegram message with ✅ Approve / ❌ Reject buttons. Nothing trades without your tap.
